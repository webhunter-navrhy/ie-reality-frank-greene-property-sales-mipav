Description

We are delighted to offer for sale this unique opportunity to acquire a home that is ready for immediate occupation. The dwelling stands on a generous site, offering the purchaser excellent potential to extend if desired. This property may qualify for the Vacant Property Refurbishment Grant, presenting an additional opportunity for prospective buyers. The home benefits from a dual heating system, including a solid fuel Stanley Range with back boiler and oil-fired central heating. Additional features include double-glazed PVC windows and a PVC front door. Externally, the property includes a number of storage sheds of varying sizes, providing ample storage and practical space. Joanstown is conveniently located approximately 10km from Edgeworthstown Town Centre, which offers a wide range of amenities including a train station, and is just 20km from Mullingar Town Centre. Viewing is highly recommended and strictly by appointment only.
